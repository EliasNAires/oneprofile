(Greenhouse 18 925 companies and Ashby 7920, `2026-10-wayback.md`). Flyway added V13 (`company.region`,

404, `jobs.eu.lever.co/kwalee` 200), with no redirect between the regions. So the regions' slugs are
disjoint as far as measured, and a company has to remember which API host serves it: ADR-0022's
region, decided on this.

## Lever's pace

Two bursts against `api.lever.co`, with slugs drawn from CC-MAIN-2025-43, as Ashby's were
(`2026-10-ashby.md` § Ashby's pace):

| Burst | 200 | 404 | 502 | Mean answer | Slowest | Downloaded |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 150 one after another | 125 | 25 | 0 | 2.99 s | 23.0 s | 91 MB |
| 300 four at a time | 229 | 70 | 1 | 2.41 s | 24.1 s | 125 MB |

Lever refused nothing, but it is slow: a 404 alone took 2 to 4 s on average, and one board answered
34 MB. At about 3 s an answer, one board at a time is about 0.33 a second, so a pause of 250 ms
between boards hardly changes a run, and Lever's own latency sets its pace; four at a time served
about 1.7 a second. A 502 came once in each of the two checks above (450 and 376 requests), so a
probe has to retry it rather than read it as not found.

## What Lever does not give

- **No company name**, as Ashby: no Lever company has a name.
- **Slugs that are no board.** Discovery reads the first path segment, so captures such as
  `jobs.lever.co/ads.txt` or `jobs.lever.co/analytics.min.js` make companies. 266 Lever slugs hold a
  dot; 212 of them probe as not found. The 54 others answer as boards whose slug is a domain
  (`amperity.com`, `allma.io`).
