# Ashby added as the second ATS, and the corpus re-swept

Run on the development machine on 2026-10-20 for #20. It starts from the dev database as it stood
after the body pass of #11 (dumped first as `archive/dev-2026-10-20-before-resweep.dump`). Flyway
added V10 (Ashby's vacancy columns, `external_id` as text) and V11 (`detected_language`) on startup.
Ashby's boards were discovered over the same twelve crawls as Greenhouse's
(`2026-09-raw-corpus.md`), newest first. Then both ATSs were probed and swept, and the whole corpus
went through cleaning, title classification, skill extraction and the body pass. Greenhouse was not
discovered again.

#11's measurement was not rerun. The counts below are what the frozen rules made of each ATS.

## Ashby's pace

Ashby refused no request in two bursts against discovered slugs: 150 one after another, at about
four a second (each answer took 0.23s on average, 0.46s at most), and 300 four at a time, at about
16 a second. The code paces Ashby at 250 ms a board for probing and sweeping alike: the four a
second it served one after another, which a run, asking one board at a time, rarely outpaces
anyway. That is a quarter of the concurrent rate, but the bursts were short (450 requests), and
the run itself is the longer test: 3332 boards probed once and swept twice without one refused.
Ashby has no answer lighter than the whole board, so a probe asks for what a sweep does.

### How far detection can be trusted

The rule (`LanguageDetectionRule`) reads the script first: Hangul, kana, Han, Hebrew, Cyrillic and
the like, once they are a fifth of the letters. Otherwise it counts each Latin-script language's
function words, and the most wins. It was run over every Greenhouse vacancy of the 2026-10-06
snapshot that declares a language, against what the board declared:

- It agrees on 175 342 of 177 067 (99.03%), and detects nothing on 204.
- Where Cyrillic text carries any letter only Ukrainian writes, it is read as Ukrainian. The 242
  Ashby `uk` are Ukrainian companies (SKELAR, Kiss My Apps, Holywater, …) writing in Ukrainian.
- On 1367 (0.77%) the two disagree about whether the body pass reads the vacancy (English or
  Spanish against anything else).
- Most disagreements read by eye are the board's declaration that is wrong: Coupang's Korean and
  Chinese descriptions, and Danish, German and French ones, declared `en`. Another part is
  bilingual text, where whichever language holds more function words wins.

## What Ashby does not give

- **No company name.** The posting API names the company nowhere, so no Ashby company has a name.
- **No time of last change**, so `updated_at` is null for every Ashby vacancy.
- **Slugs that are no board.** Discovery reads the first path segment, so a capture such as
  `jobs.ashbyhq.com/llms.txt` makes a company that probes as not found. That is part of the 940.
