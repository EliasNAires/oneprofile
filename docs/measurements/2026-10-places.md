## How a string is read

The rule (`PlaceRule`'s comment holds the whole of it) reads the longest names it knows, a code of two
or three capitals only as written, and joins the names it reads into places while some reading of them
is one place: `US, CA, Santa Clara` is one city, `Chicago, IL, Cleveland, OH` two. Of the readings of one
place the most precise wins, then the most populous city, except that a name that may be a country's is
read as the country where that fits. Four choices came from reading the strings that parsed wrong:

- **A town the list lacks, beside its state.** `Malvern, PA` read as Malvern, Ontario and Panama. A state
  or country after a city it does not hold now replaces the city (`Pennsylvania`), and a code after unread
  words is a state before a country (`Riverwoods, IL` is Illinois, not Israel). A city of a million people
  is kept instead beside an outdated state (`Hyderabad, Andhra Pradesh`, `Kuala Lumpur, Selangor`).
- **Names inside a site's name.** `Pune, Gera Commerzone SEZ` read Gera, Germany; `MUFG Global Service`
  read Worldwide. A name beside unread words, between the same commas, is read only as part of the place
  before it or as the string's first place, and never as a region.
- **A name alone that is also a subdivision's** is a city of fewer than 100,000 people only where the
  city lies in it, so `Manchester` is the English city and not the Jamaican parish, and `Taipei` the city.
- **`Anywhere` followed by a place** is that place: `Anywhere in France, Belgium, Spain` is the three
  countries and `Anywhere in Latin America` the region, not Worldwide beside them.

Where the ATS names a string's country (Ashby's address country and the country of each secondary
location), the country is taken and every name read within it, as decided on #26.

## Precision and recall

Measured once on a blind sample, against `docs/place-criterion.md`: `scripts/draw-place-sample` drew,
seeded, 300 location strings at random from the stored rows, so a string is drawn as often as vacancies
carry it, into `2026-10-place-sample/`. Two labellers of different models (Sonnet, Haiku) read every
string with the ATS's country and nothing else, and agreed on 295 of 300 (98.3%); an adjudicator settled
the rest (`disagreements/labellers/`). Where the rule and the settled labels differed, 17 strings, an
adjudicator chose between the two answers without knowing which was the rule's (`disagreements/rule/`).
`scripts/score-place-sample` scores it. A place read is **right** where it is a true place at the most
precise level the string gives; **coarser** where it agrees with a true place but less precisely
(`Burlington, WA` read as Washington); **wrong** otherwise. Recall is the share of the true places the
rule found at a level that agrees with them; read strictly, a place found only less precisely is a miss,
and recall is the last column, 96.8%.

The 9 coarser places are the 7 towns below and a country read beside a city that holds it (`China`,
`IND`). Four places of the 314 are in the wrong country. The 14 strings where the rule and the truth
differ:

- **A town the list lacks, read as its state** (coarser, 7 strings): `Burlington, WA`, `Quantico, VA`, `Rosemont
  IL`, `IL-Rosemont`, `US-HI-Waimea`, `Andover, Massachusetts, …`, `Parsdorf, Bavaria, Germany`. This is
  the fallback #26 chose; for an onsite or hybrid vacancy it is judged by country instead of distance.
- **A district read as a city** (wrong, 3): `Osterley` (in London), `Sylmar` (in Los Angeles), and
  `Manila` beside `Taguig City, National Capital Region (Manila)`.
- **A code read as a country** (wrong, 3): `EST` as Estonia (a timezone), `TG` as Togo (Telangana), `NL`
  as Newfoundland and Labrador (`Monterrey, NL` is Nuevo León).
- **`China, Hong Kong`** read as China beside the city of Hong Kong in Hong Kong, which the truth takes as
  one place, the city in China.

Easy wins the misses show, not taken: `EST` ignored, `TG` as Telangana and `NL` as Nuevo León in the hand
table, and the towns above. Whether places become a rule loop (`docs/agents/rule-loop.md`) is Elias's
decision on these figures.
