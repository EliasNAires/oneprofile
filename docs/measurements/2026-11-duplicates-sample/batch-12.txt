Il s’intègre aussi à un monitoring multi-agents qui teste et valide les optimisations proposées.
Le travail consiste à structurer un pipeline complet IA (analyse + génération), mettre en place un environnement local compatible grand contexte (plus de 20k tokens), assurer l’orchestration SLURM en période creuse, puis explorer un monitoring éventuel avec exécution et validation “live”.

# Role Overview
Security Director Cloud (SD Cloud) is a cloud-native Security Management Platform that provides centralized management, monitoring, policy administration, and automation for Juniper SRX Firewalls, vSRX, Secure Edge, and hybrid cloud security environments.
It enables enterprises to manage security policies consistently across on-premises, cloud, and remote locations through a single web interface.
SD Cloud is also positioned as HPE/Juniper's management portal for Secure Access Service Edge (SASE) and supports unified policy management, Zero Trust security, Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), threat visibility, analytics, automation, and API-driven integrations.
Security Director (On-Prem) is the on-premises version of the same security management platform.
It centrally manages SRX and vSRX firewalls, providing policy lifecycle management, monitoring, configuration, logging, and automation through a web-based interface.
It is the successor to Junos Space Security Director and offers similar operational capabilities for organizations that require local deployment.
